# Naturvidenskab og it (NatIT), Københavns Universitet: research notes

Status: **closed**. KU decided on 29 October 2014 to close the bachelor programme, effective from the 2015-16 academic year [S1]. It is not in KU's current list of science bachelor programmes [S2] or on DIKU's education page [S3]. Almost all primary material now exists only in the Wayback Machine, so most citations below point to web.archive.org.

## 1. Essence

**DA:** Naturvidenskab og it var en treårig, tværfaglig bacheloruddannelse. Den kombinerede datalogi og matematik med et naturvidenskabeligt anvendelsesfag efter eget valg, og målet var at modellere, simulere, analysere og visualisere naturvidenskabelige fænomener. KU's eget motto: ikke "it for it's skyld", men "it for naturens og menneskets skyld" [S4].

**EN:** A three-year interdisciplinary BSc. It combined computer science and mathematics with one natural-science "application subject" of the student's choice, and its goal was to train people to analyse data, build models, run simulations and visualise results for scientific phenomena [S5 §1]. The official English title was "Bachelor (BSc) in Natural Science and IT" [S5 §3].

Why it was cross-disciplinary: the core subject areas were "matematik og datalogi" plus "et individuelt valgt naturvidenskabeligt anvendelsesfag" [S5 §1 stk. 2]. It was marketed as "the only one of its kind in Denmark" in a student interview. That is a student quote, not an official claim, so treat it as UNVERIFIED [S6].

## 2. Structure

- **Length:** 3 years, 180 ECTS, title BSc [S5 §3; S7].
- **Composition** [S5 §3 stk. 1]:
  - Bachelorfagpakken: 150 ECTS, made of a 105 ECTS common mandatory part and a 45 ECTS specialisation package.
  - 30 ECTS free electives, which could be used for "kandidatadgangsfagpakker" that targeted a specific master's programme.
- **Specialisation:** chosen after year 1 [S4].
- **Hosting:** the curriculum was issued by Det Natur- og Biovidenskabelige Fakultet (SCIENCE) [S5]. In 2010 the programme was offered jointly by Det Naturvidenskabelige, Det Biovidenskabelige and Det Farmaceutiske Fakultet [S4]. Day-to-day it ran out of **DIKU (Datalogisk Institut)**:
  - DIKU announced the first graduate, and its study leader was Søren Olsen [S8].
  - DIKU recruited NatIT mentors and "rusvejledere på Naturvidenskab og it" [S9].
- **Campus:** mainly Nørre Campus, near Fælledparken [S4]. DIKU's address was Universitetsparken 1, 2100 Kbh Ø [S8].
- **Admission requirements:** Dansk A, Engelsk B, Matematik A, Fysik B, and either Kemi B or Bioteknologi A [S10; S7].

**Mandatory common courses (2010 curriculum, revised 2012)** [S5 §5 stk. 1]:
- Year 1 (all of these were "førsteårsprøvefag"):
  - Introduktion til matematik i naturvidenskab
  - Modellering i naturvidenskab
  - Lineær algebra i naturvidenskab
  - Programmering og problemløsning
  - Matematik F
  - Databaser og datamining
  - Statistiske modeller i naturvidenskab
  - Algoritmer og datastrukturer
- Year 2:
  - Introduktion til numerisk analyse
  - Datalogiens videnskabsteori
  - Numerisk løsning af differentialligninger: Differensmetoder
  - Projektkursus: Naturvidenskab og it (the "andetårsprojekt")
- Year 3: Bachelorprojekt i naturvidenskab og it (15 ECTS)
- Optional: Virksomhedsprojekt i naturvidenskab og it (15 ECTS internship) [S5 §5 stk. 9]

**The 7 specialisations, 45 ECTS each** [S5 §3 stk. 2, §5]:

| Specialisering | Kernekurser (udvalg) |
|---|---|
| Molekylærbiologi og bioteknologi | Introduktion til kemi, Biokemi, Cellebiologi, Almen mikrobiologi |
| Økologi og natur | Organismekendskab, Populationsbiologi, Almen økologi, Evolutionsbiologi, Biodiversitet, Conservation |
| Fysik | Mekanik 1–2, Termodynamik, Elektromagnetisme, Elektrodynamik og bølger, Kvantemekanik 1 |
| Geografi | Natur- og kulturlandskabet, Klima jord & vand, Naturgeografisk feltkursus, GIS & kartografi, Globale geografiske regioner |
| Geologi | Geologiske processer og materialer, Palæontologi, Sedimentologi, Danmarks geologi, Geofysik og hydrogeologi, Dynamisk stratigrafi |
| Kemi | Introduktion til kemi, Organisk kemi 1–2, Teoretisk fysisk kemi, Analytisk kemi, Proteinkemi |
| Matematik | Diskret matematik, Sandsynlighedsregning og statistik, Algebra 1, Analyse 1–2, Mål og integral |

Some of the requested science subjects were not separate tracks:
- There was **no standalone "biologi" track**. Biology was split into "molekylærbiologi og bioteknologi" and "økologi og natur".
- Pure computer science was the common core, not a specialisation.

**Projects:**
- The second-year project applied an IT solution to a real science problem. KU's own example: "modellere udviklingen af brystkræft" [S4].
- The bachelor project used a topic the student chose, and could be done with a company [S4; S7].
- Example of a real bachelor project, by the first graduate (geography track): comparing interpolation methods on annual precipitation measurements in Region Midtjylland [S8].

**Teaching:** lectures shared with datalogi, matematik, kemi and fysik students; class teaching; weekly hand-ins; lab work and fieldwork depending on track; and organised "læsegrupper" (study groups) [S4; S6].

## 3. Topic clusters (for illustration)

- **Programmering:** Programmering og problemløsning, plus algorithms and data structures [S5].
- **Matematisk modellering:** "Modellering i naturvidenskab" from block 1, year 1 [S5].
- **Simulering:** examples include cancer cells, tsunamis, fires, earthquakes and muscles in sport [S4].
- **Numerisk analyse / videnskabelig beregning:** numerical analysis and finite-difference methods for differential equations [S5].
- **Statistik og dataanalyse:** statistical models in science [S5].
- **Databaser og datamining:** a mandatory first-year course [S5].
- **Visualisering:** sea level under global warming, chemical processes [S4].
- **Lineær algebra og matematik:** the mathematical foundation [S5].
- **Bio/molekylærbiologi:** modelling biological systems and drug development [S4].
- **Økologi:** forecasting the development of a cod stock [S4].
- **Klima og geografi / GIS / remote sensing:** desertification from satellite images, GIS programming [S4; S5 §5 stk. 5.1].
- **Geologi / geofysik:** groundwater pollution, earthquakes [S11; S4].
- **Kemi:** visualising chemical reactions, computational chemistry methods [S4; S5].
- **Medicinsk billedanalyse:** modelling medical treatment from scans [S4].
- **Supercomputing / eScience:** large-scale models. The eScience Center was featured on the programme site [S4; S8].

## 4. Career paths (as stated by KU and UG at the time)

- **Generic roles, per KU** [S4; S12]:
  - "forsker i den højteknologiske industri"
  - "it-miljøspecialist i offentlige organisationer"
  - "underviser og forsker på universitetet"
  - "industriel produktudvikler inden for naturvidenskabelige områder"
- **Application domains, per UG.dk** [S7]:
  - development of new pharmaceutical products
  - weather forecasting
  - hearing-aid design
  - forecasting fish stocks in the Baltic Sea
  - consequences of global warming
- **Job functions in the curriculum:** "dataanalyse, modellering, simulation eller visualisering, inden for en bred vifte af brancher" [S5 §1].
- **Example job titles below are inferred, not sourced (UNVERIFIED):** data scientist, bioinformatician, GIS analyst, climate/hydrological modeller, research software engineer, biostatistician, scientific programmer.

**Employer panel ("aftagere") quoted by KU when the programme was designed** [S13]. These are endorsements, not documented hirers of graduates:
- Prevas: Rune Domsten, VP Technology
- Rambøll: Jens Christian Bennetsen, projektchef. He said "Rambøll vil kunne ansætte bachelorer fra alle specialiseringerne".
- IBM Danmark: Søren Damgaard, chair of the SCIENCE aftagerpanel for it og finans
- Danish Meat Research Institute / Teknologisk Institut: Lars Bager Christensen
- Novo Nordisk: Kristian Tage Hansen, chair of the aftagerpanel for sundhed og medicin. He specifically pointed to a shortage of biostatisticians.

## 5. Where graduates end up

**Master's routes:**
- Every specialisation gave access to the kandidat programmes in **Bioinformatik, Datalogi and eScience** [S4].
- Depending on track, NatIT also gave access to [S4]:
  - Biologi
  - Biologi og bioteknologi
  - Environmental and Natural Resource Economics
  - Fysik
  - Fødevarevidenskab
  - Geografi og geoinformatik
  - Geologi-geoscience
  - Kemi
  - Landskabsforvaltning
  - Lægemiddelvidenskab
  - Matematik
  - Miljøkemi og sundhed
  - Nanoscience
  - Process Analytical Technology
  - Statistik
- How many programmes that adds up to, by source:
  - "between 4 and 11 of more than 20" depending on track [S4]
  - "15-20" [S12]
  - "ca. 22" [S8]
- The first graduate chose to continue in geography [S8].

**Statistics:**
- **Graduate-specific numbers (unemployment, salary, % continuing to kandidat): NOT FOUND.** The programme produced its first graduate in January 2013 and closed to new students from 2015-16, so the cohorts were probably too small and short-lived for UG/KU dimittend statistics (UNVERIFIED).
- UG.dk cited "97 procent af de nyuddannede bachelorer inden for matematik/fysik/kemi/datalogi i beskæftigelse eller under uddannelse på højere niveau" (2009). This is a **field-level** figure that predates NatIT graduates, so do not present it as NatIT data [S7].

## 7. Notable facts, tone, culture

- **Tone of the marketing:** future-proof, big-data, "supercomputere". It asked big questions such as "Kan klimaforandringer forudsiges? Kan brystkræft forebygges bedre?" [S4].
- **Workload was a recurring theme:**
  - Studieleder Søren Olsen: many students "synes ganske vist, at det er hårdt at skulle lære at programmere", but see the payoff afterwards [S8].
  - A student: first semester had hand-ins "to gange om ugen" [S6].
- **Small and close-knit:** "et lille studium"; the first cohort cooked together in DIKU's canteen and went to Parken [S6]. Another student: "Ingen er udenfor… Vi begyndte som et lille hold" [S18].
- **DIKU culture students were connected to** [S9]:
  - The DIKU associations listed in the 2013 mentor posting: RKG, NatIT rusvejledere, "fagrådene", kantineforeningen and the revue group. A NatIT-specific fagråd name was not found (UNVERIFIED).
  - DIKU hired 4 NatIT mentors for 2013/14, against 12 for Datalogi.
- **Events:** DIKU ran "NatIT cafémøde" events. URL evidence shows one in 2012 with a CERN theme, but the content was not retrieved (UNVERIFIED) [S19].
- **Faces:** the programme site featured
  - students Anne Ulriksen Dybkjær (geography), Karina Frederiksen (aiming at molecular biomedicine) and Pia Særmark [S6; S18; S11]
  - Stefan Sommer, then a PhD student at the eScience Center, on predicting osteoarthritis from X-rays [S20]
